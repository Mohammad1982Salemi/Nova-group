Our method

Three layers, from the bottom up

Every project is built on these three layers. The upper layer does not exist without the one beneath it.

  1. Foundation

    An independent platform, built per project

    Each project gets its own data and execution platform, built from the ground up and independent of any off-the-shelf tool.

  2. Engine

    A deep order-execution simulator

    We rebuild the venue's execution engine: orders queue, wait their turn and fill in part.

  3. Intelligence

    Analytics, charts and predictive models

    From the flow of trades and orders, through statistics and model training, to a prediction algorithm.
